Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

TimeTrail does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no servers, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no network access. Everything you track — projects, time entries, tags, and settings — stays only on your Mac.

What TimeTrail stores, and where

All of your data is saved locally in a database inside the app's own sandboxed container on your Mac. It's never uploaded, synced, or shared anywhere — including to us. We have no way to see it.

  • Projects, time entries, tags, and durations you create
  • App preferences, such as your chosen theme

The widget

TimeTrail's Notification Center widget shows your current timer and today's totals. This small summary is shared between the app and the widget entirely on-device, using Apple's App Group mechanism — it never leaves your Mac.

No tracking, no ads, no accounts

TimeTrail doesn't ask you to sign in, doesn't show ads, doesn't use analytics or crash-reporting services, and doesn't track your activity in any way, in or outside the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that involves data leaving your device — this page will be updated first, with a new "Last updated" date above.
