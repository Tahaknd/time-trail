What it does

One-tap timer

Start and stop tracking in a click, from the Dock, the menu bar, or the Notification Center widget.

Projects & tags

Organize entries by project, colored for quick scanning, with free-form tags for extra context.

Reports

See totals by project and day, so you always know where your hours actually went.

Fully local

Every entry lives only on your Mac. No account, no server, nothing ever leaves your device.
